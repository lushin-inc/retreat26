Bradford Woods’ own directions page warns that GPS units often route people to the wrong place. The entrance is on the right, roughly a mile past the Marathon station, 8 miles south of Mooresville.

Logistics

Bring

  • Closed toe and closed heel shoes. Required for Friday morning’s program.
  • Layers and a jacket. Friday runs outdoors 8:30 to noon whatever the weather.
  • A refillable water bottle.
  • A camp chair, so you have a seat at the campfire both evenings.

Bedding and towels are provided — sheets, blankets, pillows, pillow cases. You just make your own bed.

Parking

Follow the yellow “Lushin Year End Retreat” signs as you enter Bradford Woods — they lead you up to the lot. Do not drive onto the grass.
